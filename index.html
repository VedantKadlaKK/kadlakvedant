Quest 01

The craft is player trust, not spectacle.

I design around the quiet contract between player and system: clear rules, readable feedback, expressive controls, and enough friction to make success feel earned.

My best work starts with the body: press, dodge, jump, swing, look, recover.

The code and AI tools are there to serve a human moment. If a player understands what happened, wants another attempt, and feels respected by the rules, the system is doing its job.

Game Feel

Movement systems, jump arcs, hit pause, cooldown rhythm, camera response, power-up feedback, and the small timings that make a prototype feel intentional.

Combat And State Logic

Hitboxes, animation states, health systems, player readability, enemy pressure, and attack windows designed around what the player can honestly perceive.

Human Input Experiments

Computer vision, MediaPipe, real-time landmark tracking, and motion streams that turn movement into playful control instead of a tech demo wearing a menu.

AI-Assisted Workflow

I use AI to explore mechanics, draft system logic, review edge cases, generate content directions, and move faster through prototype loops while keeping taste, playtesting, and final implementation grounded in human judgment.
